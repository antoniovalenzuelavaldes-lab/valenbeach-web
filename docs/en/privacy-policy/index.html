3. Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties except where legally required (for example, with the authorities responsible for traveller registration or taxation). We use WhatsApp (Meta) as a communication channel if you write to us that way, and the web hosting provider to serve this page, which may record your IP address for technical and security reasons.

For visit statistics we use GoatCounter, which does not receive your email or any data you give us by WhatsApp or email.

The availability calendar and the rates are loaded from our own service hosted on Cloudflare, which may record your IP address for technical and security reasons; it receives no personal data from you.

The email request form sends your message to our mailbox through a Cloudflare service (which only forwards it and does not store it); we receive it in our inbox, where it is kept like any other email.

4. Retention

We keep conversations and request data for as long as needed to deal with the request and, if a booking is made, for the periods required by registration, tax and commercial rules.

5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to antonio@valenbeach.com. If you believe your data is not being handled correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

6. Cookies

This site does not install cookies. Visits are measured with GoatCounter, without cookies, without advertising and without storing the IP address; for that reason it does not show a cookie banner. If your browser sends a "do not track" signal, your visit is not even counted.
