General booking conditions

Courtesy translation of the Spanish original. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

These conditions govern the short-stay rental of the apartment Valen Beach in Torrox Costa (Málaga), official accommodation registration number AT-434842-A. They are complemented by the legal notice and the privacy policy.

1. Who rents

Antonio Valenzuela Valdes, tax ID (NIF) 74662870V, with address at Carretera de Almería 23b, bloque 7, bajo A, 29770 Torrox (Málaga). Contact: antonio@valenbeach.com · (+34) 615 174 997 (also by WhatsApp).

2. Request and confirmation of the booking

Requests are made by WhatsApp, email or telephone. A request is not a booking: the booking is confirmed when the owner accepts it in writing and the deposit indicated below is received. The maximum capacity of the accommodation is 10 people. Pets are not allowed.

3. Price

  • The price is per night and includes VAT. Current rates are shown on the home page.
  • The minimum stay is 2 nights.
  • Final cleaning is included in the price, as are bed linen, towels and wifi.
  • An electricity allowance of 50 kWh per week is included.

4. Deposit and payment

To confirm the booking, a deposit of €50 is paid, which is deducted from the final price. The rest of the price is paid in the way and on the date agreed when the booking is confirmed. Payment methods are communicated on confirmation; they are not published on this website.

5. Security deposit

Before arrival the guest hands over a security deposit of €400 as a guarantee. It is returned after checking the condition of the apartment, on the day of departure or at the latest within the following 3 days. Any damage or additional charges under section 10 will be deducted from the security deposit where applicable.

6. Cancellation

The cancellation policy is flexible: you can cancel up to 1 week (7 days) before the arrival date. To cancel, just notify us by WhatsApp or email. If you cancel within that week, what was agreed when the booking was confirmed applies.

7. Right of withdrawal

As this is an accommodation service with a specific date of performance, the 14-day statutory right of withdrawal does not apply (Article 103(l) of the Spanish consolidated text of the General Law for the Protection of Consumers and Users). The cancellation policy in section 6 applies.

8. Arrival and departure

  • Arrival: from 4:00 pm to 9:00 pm on the day of arrival. Earlier or later arrival must be agreed in advance and may carry an extra charge.
  • Departure: by 11:00 am at the latest on the day of departure. A later departure must be agreed in advance and may carry an extra charge.
  • Check-in is self-service: the arrival instructions are sent to the guest once the booking is confirmed.
  • On leaving, the guest leaves the apartment locked with the keys inside, and notifies us by WhatsApp.

9. Guest data

Before arrival the guest must send the name and ID document of all occupants, and the registration number of the vehicle using the parking space. This is a legal traveller-registration obligation; see the privacy policy.

10. Additional charges

  • Electricity: consumption above 50 kWh per week is charged at €0.45 per kWh. The meter can be read next to the front door.
  • Arrival or departure outside the agreed hours: on request.
  • Cot: €1 per day.
  • Lost keys: the physical key is copy-protected and losing it means replacing the whole lock (€150). Losing a magnetic key costs €20.
  • Additional services, with price and availability on request: bicycle hire, and hire of parasol and beach chairs.

11. Rules of use

  • Smoking is not allowed in the apartment.
  • Parties are strictly forbidden in the community. If a dinner runs past midnight, it must continue inside the apartment.
  • Rubbish must be put in the bin outside the residential complex before departure.
  • The rules of the owners' community must be followed; they are given to the guest.
  • Air conditioning must be used in moderation and only when you are at home.
  • The towel sets are for indoor use and must not be taken to the beach.

12. Guest's responsibility

The guest is responsible for any damage caused to the apartment, the furniture or the common areas, and for breaches of the community rules. The owner may terminate the stay if the rules of use are seriously breached.

13. Applicable law and complaints

Spanish law. For any complaint, write to antonio@valenbeach.com. If you are a consumer, the courts of your place of residence have jurisdiction.
