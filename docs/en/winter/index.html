Winter in Torrox Costa: a large apartment for groups and families

In winter the Costa del Sol is a quiet destination with a mild climate and none of the summer crowds. Valen Beach is a 4-bedroom apartment for up to 10 people, less than 100 m from the beach in Torrox Costa, made for families and groups who want to spend a few days together.

Why choose Torrox Costa in winter

  • A mild climate compared with northern Europe, ideal for walks by the sea.
  • A beach and seafront promenade without the crowds.
  • Bars and restaurants within 500 m of the apartment.
  • White villages and mountains a short drive away for day trips.
  • Heating and wifi, so you stay comfortable when it gets cooler.

Who it is for

  • Families who want to spend Christmas, New Year or Epiphany together.
  • Get-togethers of friends or several generations: there is room for 10 people.
  • Groups who combine sea and mountains: hiking, cycling or simply resting.
  • Anyone who needs to work for a few days with a good connection: wifi included.

What is included

  • 4 bedrooms with air conditioning, heating and room for 10 people.
  • Wifi, bed linen, towels and final cleaning are included in the price.
  • Terrace, washing machine, dishwasher and private parking.
  • Self check-in: we send the arrival instructions when the booking is confirmed.

Getting here and what is nearby

  • Málaga airport is about 64 km away.
  • Nerja, famous for the Balcón de Europa and the Nerja Caves, is about 5 km away.
  • White villages such as Frigiliana are close by, perfect for a winter day trip.
  • Las Dunas beach, next to the protected Dunas de la Carraca area, is less than 100 m away.

Frequently asked questions

How many people does it sleep and what are the rooms like?

Up to 10 guests: four bedrooms and two bathrooms on a single floor, with air conditioning and heating.

What is included in the price?

The nightly price includes VAT, final cleaning, bed linen, towels and wifi. The minimum stay is 2 nights.

Which airport should I fly into?

The nearest airport is Málaga, about 64 km away. A cot is available for an extra charge.

Price

From €200 per night for the whole apartment, with VAT and final cleaning included and a minimum stay of 2 nights. With 10 people that is about €20 per person per night. Check the free dates in the calendar and write to us if you want to book over Christmas or New Year.
